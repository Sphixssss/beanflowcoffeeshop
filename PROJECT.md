# Project Context

## Repository
https://github.com/Sphixssss/beanflowcoffeeshop

## Deployment
Not deployed yet.

## Product
BeanFlow Coffee Shop online ordering and management system.

## Audience
Customers, especially students and young professionals, plus coffee shop administrators.

## Goal
Make online ordering the primary customer experience while supporting account management, order history, inventory, transactions, sales reports, product management, and AI-assisted promotion recommendations.

## Brand
Warm, modern, friendly, convenient, and trustworthy. Coffee brand first, technology second.

## Visual Direction
Warm coffee-inspired palette, clean modern typography, generous spacing, focused composition, subtle depth, minimal clutter, responsive design.

## Page Structure
Hero, customer capabilities, menu, ordering flow, AI promotion section, about section, business tools, final CTA, footer.

## Copy Direction
Direct, specific, useful, human. Focus on easy ordering and convenience. Avoid generic marketing filler and exaggerated claims.

## Assets
No external brand assets provided. Current visuals are CSS-built and need no image files.

## Functionality
Prototype includes menu category filters, add-to-cart, quantity controls, cart drawer, mock sign-in/register modal, and checkout placeholder. Real authentication, payment, inventory persistence, reporting, and AI logic are future integrations.

## Avoid
Clutter, excessive animation, excessive color variety, tiny text, complicated navigation, and technology-heavy customer-facing presentation.

## Current Decisions
- Primary CTA: Order Now.
- Secondary CTA: View Menu.
- Primary roles: Customer and Administrator.
- Core categories: Coffee, Non-Coffee, Pastries.
- Ordering flow: Browse -> Add to Cart -> Review Cart -> Checkout -> Order Confirmation.
- AI promotion recommendations are positioned as an admin/business advantage.
